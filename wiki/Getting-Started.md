# Getting Started

New to Nerospace? Follow these steps in order. Each step tells you what to get and what to build next.
Click any link for the full page.

> **Tip:** craft a **[Star Guide Book](Star-Guide)** (Book + Raw Nerosium, or Book + a Nerosium Ore
> block) as soon as you can. It is this
> guide inside the game and always shows your next step.

## Step 1 — Find Nerosium

- **Where:** in the Overworld, underground, between **y −24 and y 56**. It is about as common as iron.
- **Tool:** an **iron pickaxe** or better.
- **What you get:** **Raw Nerosium**.
  - If you get the **ore block** instead, that's OK: smelt the ore block into an ingot, grind it into
    2 dust, or use it for the Star Guide Book. (Normally this only happens with **Silk Touch** — if
    it happens without, please tell us on Discord.)
  - **JEI doesn't show mining drops.** Raw Nerosium only comes from breaking the ore, so you won't
    find a crafting recipe for it.
- **Next:** smelt Raw Nerosium in a furnace to get **Nerosium Ingots**.

## Step 2 — Find Nerosteel

You need Nerosteel for almost every machine, including every generator. Get it early.

- **Where:** in the Overworld, **deep down**, between **y −56 and y 16**. It is rarer than Nerosium.
- **Tool:** an **iron pickaxe** or better.
- **What you get:** **Raw Nerosteel**. Smelt it into **Nerosteel Ingots**.
- Collect a lot — roughly **60 ingots** gets you to the first planet.

## Step 3 — Make power

Machines do not make their own power. You need a generator first.

- **[Combustion Generator](Combustion-Generator):** 7 Nerosteel Ingots + Furnace + Redstone. Burns coal.
- **[Passive Generator](Passive-Generator):** 7 Nerosteel Ingots + Block of Nerosium + Redstone. Burns
  one Raw Nerosium for 20 minutes.

Place the generator **touching** your machine. It sends power by itself — no cable needed.
Read **[Powering Machines](Powering-Machines)** if a machine shows 0% power.

## Step 4 — Build a Nerosium Grinder

- **Recipe:** 5 Nerosium Ingots + Furnace + 3 Cobblestone.
- Put Raw Nerosium in → get **2 Nerosium Dust**. Smelt each dust into an ingot.
  That is **double** the ingots compared to smelting raw.
- Put a generator next to it, or it will stay at **0% power**.

See **[Nerosium Grinder](Nerosium-Grinder)**.

## Step 5 — Make rocket fuel

Every fuel recipe needs **Blaze Powder**, so go to the Nether and get some blaze rods first.

- **Easy way:** craft **Rocket Fuel Canisters** — Blaze Powder + Coal + Iron Ingot gives 2 canisters.
  Each canister holds 1,000 mB of fuel.
- **Machine way:** a **[Fuel Refinery](Fuel-Refinery)** turns 1 coal + 1 blaze powder into 2,000 mB of
  fuel. Store it in a **[Fuel Tank](Fuel-Tank)**.

## Step 6 — Build a rocket and a launch pad

- **[Launch Pad](Rocket-Launch-Pad):** 8 Nerosteel Ingots + Block of Nerosium.
- **Tier 1 Rocket:** 5 Nerosteel Ingots + Block of Nerosteel + Rocket Fuel Canister.
- A Tier 1 rocket only needs **one** launch pad block.
- Fill the rocket with fuel: right-click it with canisters.
- A trip to another world costs **2,500 mB** of fuel. **Bring at least 2 extra canisters** to fly home.

## Step 7 — Get ready to breathe

Every place you can fly to has **no air**. Without a suit you run out of air in about 5 seconds.

- **[Oxygen Suit](Oxygen-Suit) (all four pieces):** made from Nerosteel Ingots. A full suit gives you
  about **2½ minutes** of air.
- You can breathe near a **launch pad** or **landing pod** (within 6 blocks), and inside the air bubble
  of an **[Oxygen Generator](Oxygen-Generator)**.
- Standing near an Oxygen Generator or a gas tank with oxygen refills your suit.

## Step 8 — Fly to space

| Rocket | Pad you need | Where it can go | New things there |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | 1 pad block | Orbital Station | A safe base in space |
| Tier 2 | Full 3×3 pad | + **Greenxertz** | Lots of Nerosteel, **Xertz Quartz**, alien villages |
| Tier 3 | 3×3 pad with a ring of Station Walls | + **Cindara** | **Cindrite** (very hot — bring a Thermal Suit) |
| Tier 4 | Heavy Launch Complex (with a [Launch Gantry](Launch-Gantry)) | + **Glacira** | **Glacite** (very cold — bring a Cryo Suit) |

Each new rocket is made from the one before it. The Orbital Station already has a 3×3 pad, so you can
launch a Tier 2 rocket from there.

## Step 9 — Better gear

- **Solar Panels** (need Xertz Quartz) — free power from the sun.
- **Thermal Suit** (Cindrite) for Cindara, **Cryo Suit** (Glacite) for Glacira. Without them you lose
  air 4× faster.
- **[Quarry Controller](Quarry-Controller)** — mines a whole area for you. See the
  [Quarry Setup Guide](Quarry-Setup-Guide).
- **[Universal Pipes](Universal-Pipe)** — move power, fuel, oxygen and items between machines.

## Step 10 — End game

- **[Station Charter](Station-Charter)** — build your own space stations.
- **[Cargo Pad](Cargo-Pad) and [Cargo Rocket](Cargo-Rocket)** — send items between bases automatically.
- **[Terraformer](Terraformer)** — turn a dead planet into green land with air, water and animals.
  You need Glacite for water, so get to Glacira first.

## Stuck?

- **Machine at 0% power** → [Powering Machines](Powering-Machines).
- **Got ore blocks instead of raw items** → your pickaxe has Silk Touch. Smelt the block, or grind it.
- **Can't find Nerosteel** → dig lower, below y 16. It is much more common on Greenxertz.
- **Rocket won't launch** → not enough fuel, or the pad is too small for that rocket tier.
- Still stuck? Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/ArPXvYUzJG).
