# Europa

Europa is a **Tier 4** destination: a cracked white ice shell over a dark hidden ocean, under a giant Jupiter that
never moves in the sky. It is airless, frigid and irradiated on top, milder under the ice, and warm only beside
the vents on the sea floor.

## Getting there

Europa is on the Tier 4 destination list, after Nyxara. Fly a Tier 4 rocket from a
[Heavy Launch Complex](Launch-Gantry) and you land on the ice surface near the middle of the world. There is no
other way in: the destination needs no extra progress beyond the rocket tier. See [Rockets](Rockets).

## The three layers

Europa is built in three layers, one above the other.

| Layer | Height | Regions | What you find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Surface** | Y 176 to 212, and the first blocks below it | Europa Ice Plains, Chaos Terrain, Lineae, Penitente Field | White Europa Ice; tilted rafts of Chaos Ice; rust-red Lineae Salt ridges; fields of Penitente Blades. Cryovents and Oxidant Ice. Ice fractures (shafts about two blocks wide) cut down into the shell |
| **Ice shell** | from about Y 80 up to the surface layer | Ice Shell | Solid Shell Ice, 76 to 128 blocks thick, with Deuterium Ice, Brine Salt Ore and pockets of Brine. Where the underside of the shell rises above sea level (Y 88) there are air domes over the water; the Brinekin build there |
| **Ocean** | sea level Y 88 down to the sea floor (Y -44 to -20) | Abyss, Vent Fields | At least 104 blocks of dark water under the ice roof. The floor is Vent Basalt. The deepest part, the Vent Fields, has Hydrothermal Vents, Smoker Chimneys, Tube Mats and Vent Sulfide Ore |

Europa has its own terrain shape, with no caves and no carvers: you get into the shell by digging or by
following a fracture down. The ocean does not reach the surface anywhere until you
[terraform](#terraforming-and-fauna) it.

## Hazards and protection

Europa has no air: you always need an [Oxygen Suit](Oxygen-Suit) or a sealed, oxygenated room. What else bites
depends on where you are:

| Where | Cold | Radiation | You need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open surface | frigid | level 2: one point of damage every two seconds without a lining | oxygen, a full **Cryo Suit**, **Rad Lining** |
| Surface layer, with shading ice in the three blocks above your head | frigid | level 1: no damage | oxygen, a Cryo Suit; without a lining your oxygen still drains four times as fast |
| Open surface in a radiation storm | frigid | level 3: two points every two seconds without a lining | as the open surface; the lining still protects fully |
| The first 8 to 44 blocks under the surface | frigid | level 1 under the ice above you (as the row above) | a Cryo Suit: this still counts as the surface layer |
| Deeper in the ice shell, and the ocean | mild | none | oxygen and any Tier 2 suit (Tier 2, Thermal or Cryo) |
| Within five blocks of an active vent, or two of warm Vent Basalt | none | as the layer | oxygen (and the lining on the surface) |

- Only a few blocks shade radiation: Europa Ice, Chaos Ice, Cut Europa Ice, Europa Ice Bricks and Shell Ice. The
  shade counts only within **three blocks above your head**, so a tall ice hall is unshaded in its middle. Keep
  ceilings low or carry the lining.
- The bottom of an open fracture can see the sky. In a radiation storm it counts as exposed: no damage, but an
  unlined suit drains faster.
- Water is never breathable, and neither is the sea of a terraformed Europa.
- Inside a sealed, oxygenated room no hazard applies. Europa Ice, Chaos Ice, Cut Europa Ice, Europa Ice Bricks,
  Shell Ice and the three Vent Basalt blocks all seal an oxygen room.
- Do not build a base from plain Europa Ice: it melts next to a vent, a lit campfire or lava, and a Terraformer
  converts it. Use Cut Europa Ice, Europa Ice Bricks or the basalt blocks.
- The planet card on the rocket screen reads **Cryo+Rad** for Europa.

## Clock and weather

A Europa day is 85 200 ticks. The sun is small and weak (solar panels peak at about 4.25 and get almost nothing
in the long night). Every noon the sun passes behind Jupiter: for a short while it goes nearly dark, and the HUD
clock dial marks that window as *Eclipse*. At night Jupiter is full and gives a faint light. **Beds** work in the
Europa night as on Mars. See [Planet Clock and Weather](Planet-Clock-and-Weather).

Europa has two kinds of weather:

- **Plume fall**: ice from a geyser plume settles back as slow, glittering flakes, and a pale veil hangs in the
  sky. It is harmless, but open water under the sky skins over with ice. Up to about three times a Europa day, lasting
  2.5 to 7.5 minutes. The HUD weather line calls it *Diamond dust*.
- **Radiation storm**: Europa crosses Jupiter's magnetic tail. The open surface goes from radiation level 2 to 3,
  violet motes drift, the air crackles, and aurora curtains light the sky. It can only begin around noon, so it
  often runs through the eclipse. About one every two Europa days, lasting 1.5 to 3 minutes. The HUD weather line
  calls it *Solar flare*.

Both are switched off together with the server setting `weather.europa.enabled` (see
[Configuration](Configuration)).

**Open water freezes** under the sky on the natural surface. Brine never freezes, so a vent under a Brine pool
stays active at the surface; under the ice any water keeps a vent going.

## Sky

A black sky full of stars, day and night. **Jupiter** hangs huge and fixed high in the south, showing phases as
the sun moves round, and its cloud bands drift slowly while the great red spot stays put. Little Io and
Ganymede cross the sky with their own phases. At noon the sun slips behind Jupiter and an orange ring lights up
around its edge. Plume falls hang a pale veil over the northern sky; radiation storms bring violet aurora.

The band drift is drawn only on full sky quality; on lite quality Jupiter is a still picture. The drift also
works with the square body style, where Jupiter is a flat, detailed square. The full sky is drawn on Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 (NeoForge, Fabric and
Forge). On the 1.21 versions Europa shows its colours with the vanilla sky shapes. Shader packs get their End
look here; see [Shaders](Shaders).

## The Brinekin

Europa's **[villagers](Alien-Villagers)** are the **Brinekin**: under-ice people with pale blue-grey skin,
rust-salt markings and a glowing cyan throat. They never go up to the surface. They build their habitats at sea
level inside the ice shell: a lamp-lit platform in an air dome where the shell rises above the water, or a
chamber carved into the ice where it does not. They build with Europa Ice Bricks, Chaos Ice, Polished Vent Basalt
and Cut Europa Ice, lit by sea lanterns behind tinted glass. They buy Brine Salt and sell ice bricks, Brine
Buckets, Deuterium Shards and Cells, Oxidant Crystals, Tube Mats, a cold-protection helmet and Rad Lining; at a
high trust tier they also sell a Hydrothermal Vent, the only way to get one without Silk Touch. They have no
Mega-City. Their habitats appear only in newly generated land. See [Alien Structures](Alien-Structures).

## Terraforming and fauna

The [Terraformer](Terraformer) works on the surface:

1. **Rooted** (*Softened Ice*): the ice turns to snow over clean ice; Penitente Blades and loose snow are cleared.
2. **Hydrated** (*Open Leads*): one spring opens in each chunk, at the lowest ground the Terraformer has watered.
3. **Living** (*Europan Sea*): the top four blocks become open water, a shallow sea on top of the shell. A
   starter school of Lantern Drifters is stocked in the water.

Terraformed Europa ends in **open ocean, not in a breathable world**: you still need oxygen and a Tier 2 suit, but
no longer a Cryo Suit, and the radiation drops to a level that does no damage (except in the open during a radiation
storm, so keep the lining). The terraformed sea does not freeze (water you place far above it, higher than about Y 230, still can). Gravity on terraformed chunks is 1.0, all the
way down. Under a Terraformer the biomes below the surface change too: the shell, ocean and vent fields under that
column become the terraformed biome, so Vent Rays spawn there only once it is **Living**. The shallow sea does
not join the real ocean 80 blocks below; dig down or follow a fracture if you want them joined. Plain Europa Ice
inside the Terraformer's reach is converted, so build with cut ice or bricks.

Europa's native creatures (all passive, see [Creatures](Creatures)):

- **Lantern Drifter**: a glowing bell with trailing ribbons. It pulses through the ocean, rises toward light and
  active vents, and gathers in loose schools. Drops glow ink sacs.
- **Vent Ray**: a flat manta of the deep vent fields (below Y 0). It circles an active vent, settles beside it
  for a while, and flicks away when you come within three blocks. Drops Brine Salt.
- **Shell Skater**: a low six-legged ice strider that glides across ice, licks Lineae Salt and steps around
  Penitente Blades. It lives on the surface and in the dark ice shell. Breed it with Brine Salt; it drops Brine
  Salt.

## Star Guide

**Worlds: Europa**: land, mine Deuterium Ice for a shard, place Cut Europa Ice, last two minutes under the open sky
through a plume fall or a radiation storm (under the ice shell there is no weather, so it does not count), and
stand on ice your Terraformer has softened. See [Star Guide](Star-Guide).

## More

- **Quests**: with NeroQuests installed, the **Worlds Apart** chapter gains four Europa quests: *Under the
  Giant*, *Heavy Water*, *Tail Crossing* and *Open Water*.
- **Meteors**: Europa adds Deuterium Shards (rare) and Brine Salt (common) to the meteor materials that
  [Neroland Core](Neroland-Core)'s meteors can carry.
- **Map layer**: with a Neroland map, the **Habitats** layer marks alien village cores within 64 blocks of you,
  on any world (see [Mod Compatibility](Mod-Compatibility)).
- The **Europa Compass** (creative only) takes you straight to Europa, like the other compasses.

## Tips

- Bring a Rad-Lined Cryo Suit for the surface; once you are deep in the shell or the ocean a Tier 2 suit is enough.
- Warm a base with a Hydrothermal Vent: put Brine (at the surface) or water (under the ice) on top of it.
- Mine Deuterium Ice away from your vents and campfires, or it gives nothing.
- Leave Oxidant Ice under the open sky for a while: a fully charged block gives four crystals.
- Mine Vent Sulfide Ore under water, or you only get Sulfur.
- Listen for the cracking of Shell Ice: it warns you before you dig into a Brine pocket or the ocean.
- Solar power is weak and dies at night: bring Deuterium Cells for the Combustion Generator.
